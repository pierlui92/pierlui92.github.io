## Example topics

The list below is indicative: topics evolve with ongoing projects, and I am happy to discuss your own ideas.

* **Neural fields and Gaussian Splatting meet language models**: understanding, retrieving and describing 3D scenes directly from their neural representations.
* **Very high-resolution images**: semantic segmentation of very high-resolution images, compression and super-resolution of gigapixel images.
* **3D and multimodal anomaly detection** for industrial quality control.
* **Depth estimation** for challenging scenes, such as transparent and reflective objects.
* **Implicit neural representations**: new architectures and activations for representing signals.
* **Learning with limited labels**: domain adaptation, self-supervised learning and synthetic data.

## How to apply

Send an email to [pier.zamaramirez@unive.it](mailto:pier.zamaramirez@unive.it) with:

1. your **CV** and the **list of exams** with grades;
2. a few lines on **which topics** interest you and why;
3. your **expected period** and availability (e.g., months, part-time or full-time).

Familiarity with Python is required; experience with PyTorch and a background in machine learning or computer vision are a plus, but not mandatory.
